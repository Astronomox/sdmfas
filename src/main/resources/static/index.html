COS202 · GROUP 9 · CASE STUDY: LAGOS STATE

Detect drainage failures before they become floods.

SDMFAS gives residents, commuters, and businesses across Lagos State a direct channel to report blocked drains and flooded spots — routed automatically to the responsible LGA, with a public dashboard flagging hotspots in real time.

Report an incident View public alerts

The problem

Blocked and poorly maintained drains flood roads across Lekki, Ajah, Apapa, Iyana-Ipaja, Agege and beyond every rainy season. Reports, where they happen at all, are informal and untraceable — small blockages that could be cleared in a day go unattended until they cause serious damage.

How it works

One platform, from street report to public response.

1

Report

A resident submits a location, photo, and short description of a blockage or flooded spot.

2

Route

The report is automatically tagged to the responsible Local Government Area — no manual sorting.

3

Alert

Once an LGA has 3 or more unresolved reports, it's flagged as a hotspot on the public dashboard.

Built for two audiences

The same reporting data serves both sides of the problem.

For residents and commuters

Check the public alert dashboard before you travel, and see which routes have known flooding or drainage issues before you set out.

For LGA authorities

Review every incoming report before it counts publicly — verify legitimate issues, reject spam or duplicates, and get a prioritized, location-tagged list with photo evidence so response effort goes where it's actually needed.
