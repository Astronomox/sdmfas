COS202 · GROUP 9

Smart Drainage Monitoring and Flood Alert System

A community-driven digital platform for flood detection, reporting, and response.

Project objectives

What SDMFAS sets out to do, in order.

  1. 1Give residents, commuters, and businesses across Lagos State an easy way to report blocked drains and flooded areas using location, photos, and incident details.
  2. 2Ensure reports reach the appropriate Local Government Area authority promptly, with enough information to act.
  3. 3Give the public a reliable way to check current flood alerts and updates before travelling.
  4. 4Generate data on recurring flood-prone locations that can guide preventive maintenance and long-term infrastructure planning.

Expected outcomes

What success looks like once this is deployed beyond the prototype stage.

Faster

Quicker identification and clearing of blocked drains before they cause flooding.

Safer

Reduced risk to pedestrians and motorists through advance flood alerts.

Smarter

A documented, data-backed record of flood-prone areas to support long-term planning.

Stronger

Better collaboration between residents and the LGA authorities responsible for drainage maintenance.

Scope and approach

What the prototype actually covers.

  1. •Reference site: Lagos State, covering its 20 constituent Local Government Areas.
  2. •Core users: Residents, commuters, businesses, and LGA / state drainage-works authorities.
  3. •Core features: Incident reporting with location, photo and description; automated routing to the responsible LGA; a public flood-status alert view.

Alignment with the Sustainable Development Goals

The two SDGs this project directly supports.

11

Sustainable Cities and Communities

Improving drainage maintenance response and flood management across Lagos supports safer, more resilient, and better-managed urban infrastructure state-wide.
